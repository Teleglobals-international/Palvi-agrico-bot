What It Takes To Run It

  • A contact database — Phone numbers and customer data must be in the system — not in spreadsheets.
  • Telephony connected once — Any provider (Exotel, Twilio, Ozonetel). No manual dialing after that.
  • Multilingual STT/TTS configured — Set it up one time. Handles regional languages and dialects automatically.
  • Call scripts defined — Business team defines the script. Bot follows with zero drift.

Who It's For

  • Outbound sales & support teams Replaces a telecalling team. 1,000+ calls/day with consistent delivery, zero fatigue.
  • Not a replacement for complex negotiations Bot qualifies leads, pitches offers, collects info. Humans close complex deals.
  • Campaign execution at scale Promotions, reminders, surveys, renewals — one script change, thousands of calls.
